TimesMissionAttempted is a plain NOTSA_GLOBAL now.
* **Shopping**: GET_ITEM_IN_SHOP / GET_NUMBER_OF_ITEMS_IN_SHOP / GET_LOADED_SHOP (Group18.cpp / Group19.cpp, files of other batches, unmodified by others at the time) read three private CShopping statics through StaticRef literals = strays after the conversion. * TaskSimpleCarGetIn.cpp: comments said 30.f / 0.001f; the exe image (so the code) is 10.0f / 0.01f. Comments corrected.
* TheScripts.cpp (B15, D-B14, uncommitted when I worked) has two `assert((void*)0xA49960 == ...)` lines in `#ifndef NOTSA_STANDALONE`: compiled out in standalone, now exempt in the checker; I did not touch the file. TheScripts.h edits are compatible with either state of that .cpp.
